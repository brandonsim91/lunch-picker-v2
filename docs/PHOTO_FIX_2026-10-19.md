# Google Maps photo repair — 19 October 2026

At baseline commit 60b520e, 29 of the 50 restaurants introduced by 67d06fe had no Google Place ID. PlacePhoto requests /api/places by internal restaurant ID; the route looks up the local catalogue and returns image:null without calling Google when placeId is missing. A live Barcook Bakery photo request succeeded before the fix, confirming the production photo service works for an existing ID.

Added 27 Place IDs after reviewing Google Maps business names and outlet addresses. Evidence is in PLACE_ID_VERIFICATION_2026-10-19.json. These are public Google Maps listing matches, not automatic first-result acceptance. Only placeId changed in the restaurant data. The API still verifies OPERATIONAL status, the photo resource's place prefix and the image host/type/size before returning bytes and attribution; its existing fallbacks remain intact.
